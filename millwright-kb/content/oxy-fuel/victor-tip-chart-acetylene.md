All pressures are **psig at the regulator with the torch flowing**. Victor measured cutting-tip values through a 25 ft × 3/8" hose for tip 6 and larger. Torches fitted with flashback arrestors can need **up to 25% more pressure** as tip size goes up, but acetylene still tops out at **15 psig**.

\* Preheat-oxygen column applies to **3-hose machine torches only**. On a 2-hose hand torch the preheat pressure is set by the cutting-oxygen setting.
\*\* Tip 6 and larger: use a heavy-duty torch and 3/8" hose.

**Caution (from the card):** never draw more than 1/7 of an acetylene cylinder's contents per hour. If you need more flow, manifold cylinders.

### Reading a Victor cutting-tip number

`1-1-101` = tip size **1**, fuel type **1** (acetylene), series **101** (standard hand-cutting). A `0-3-101` is size 0 in the 3-101 series (same chart, different seat length for other torches). GPN/HPN series tips are for **propane/natural gas** and use a different chart; do not use them on acetylene.

## MFA heating nozzles (rosebuds)

| Tip size | Acetylene (psig) | Oxygen (psig) | Acetylene (scfh) min-max | Oxygen (scfh) min-max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 6-10 | 8-12 | 6-20 | 7-22 |
| 6 | 8-12 | 10-15 | 14-40 | 15-44 |
| 8 | 10-15 | 20-30 | 30-80 | 33-88 |
| 10 | 12-15 | 30-40 | 40-100 | 44-110 |
| 12* | 12-15 | 50-60 | 60-150 | 66-165 |
| 15* | 12-15 | 50-60 | 90-220 | 99-244 |

\* Sizes 12 and 15 need the HD310C torch and 3/8" hose. Acetylene has roughly 1 470 gross BTU per cubic foot. A size 8 rosebud pulling 80 scfh needs at least **560 ft³ of acetylene cylinder capacity** (80 × 7) to respect the 1/7 rule, which usually means a manifold of two or more #4 (approx. 300 ft³) cylinders.

## Notes for students

- Pressures on the card are **starting points**. Adjust for a clean neutral flame and a clean cut; do not chase the numbers.
- When the shop torch has a **flashback arrestor** on the handle, set toward the top of each range.
- Keep the printed Victor card in your toolbox. Every school and every Victor dealer hands out the same one.
